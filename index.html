An unaffiliated, non-commercial hobby reproduction of the mechanics of Riftbound: The League of Legends TCG. Not endorsed by or affiliated with Riot Games. All artwork here is this project's own.

Choose your legend

Ten tournament-winning lists, one per legend. Your rival's deck is drawn from the seed.

How to play

Win by reaching 8 points. You score by controlling battlefields: one point for each battlefield you still hold at the start of your turn (hold), and one for each you take from the opponent (conquer). The eighth point can only be taken by holding, or by conquering every battlefield in a single turn.

Two currencies. Exhausting a ready rune gives 1 Energy. Recycling a rune — sending it back to your rune deck — gives 1 Power of that rune's domain. You channel 2 runes every turn. Payment is handled for you.

Units enter exhausted in your base. Moving a unit exhausts it. Moving into a battlefield you do not control contests it, which opens a showdown, and a showdown with units on both sides becomes a combat: both sides deal damage equal to their total might, and whoever still has units standing takes the battlefield.
