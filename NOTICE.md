# NOTICE

**BreachForge** is a non-commercial, unaffiliated hobby reproduction of the *mechanics* of
**Riftbound: The League of Legends TCG**, made for one person to practise decks against an AI.
It is not published by, endorsed by, sponsored by or affiliated with **Riot Games, Inc.**
Riftbound, League of Legends and the champion names are trademarks of Riot Games.

- **Nothing here is sold, monetised or advertised.**
- **No official artwork or audio is in this repository.** Every image the game draws is the
  project's own — procedurally generated, or generated from original prose prompts.
- Card names and printed rules text are mechanical reference, carried in one generated pack
  (`data/printed.js`) that can be switched off in the UI and deleted in a single commit.
- Card and deck data is derived from the community mirror
  [`LouisCourrian/riftbound-cards`](https://github.com/LouisCourrian/riftbound-cards) and from
  publicly posted tournament decklists. See `docs/sources.md`.

Riot publishes a fan-content policy (the "Legal Jibber Jabber") and a separate **Riftbound TCG
Digital Tools Policy**. Both were read on **2026-09-20** and are summarised in `docs/sources.md`.

**Read plainly, they do not permit this project to be published.** The general policy prohibits
using Riot IP in games and apps. The Riftbound policy prohibits standalone clients solely for
Riftbound, and prohibits apps that create a digital gameplay experience with automated rule
enforcement — which is what this engine is. It also requires that card assets come from the Riot
API rather than a community mirror. The licence either policy grants is revocable at Riot's
discretion, for any reason or none.

BreachForge is therefore kept as a private practice tool, run locally and not deployed. It is
recorded here rather than left implicit so that nobody has to rediscover it.
